Venture Building

Some businesses shouldn't start with a business plan.

They should start with a problem worth solving. OM4Biz identifies opportunities, develops business models and builds the infrastructure required to turn promising ideas into operating businesses — the same process behind all six businesses in our own portfolio.

Our model

From idea to operating business.

01 — Identify
Find a genuine market problem, opportunity or structural gap.
02 — Validate
Test the market, customer demand, economics and competitive landscape.
03 — Design
Build the business model, proposition, operating structure and technology requirements.
04 — Build
Develop the brand, systems, technology, processes and operating infrastructure.
05 — Launch
Take the business to market and establish the operating engine.
06 — Operate
Run, optimise and improve the business.
07 — Scale
Expand the customer base, markets, capabilities and economics.
