About OM4Biz

We don't just advise businesses. We build and operate them.

Founded in 2006 — 20 years of business building and operating experience, distinct from the founder's own 25+ years in the field.

Strategy is easy to sell. Execution is where businesses succeed or fail. OM4Biz works at the intersection of strategy, operations, technology and customer experience — helping businesses move from opportunity to execution, and from execution to sustainable growth.

Founded in 2006, OM4Biz has grown from a BPO and business consulting practice into a Dubai-based operating and venture-building group — developing and running businesses across real estate advisory, interior fit-out, technology, F&B and media, unified by the same operational discipline and customer-experience thinking.

OM4Biz is not simply a holding company. It is an operating platform through which businesses are built, developed and managed across different markets and sectors — some fully operated, some advised, some still early-stage. See how the portfolio breaks down →
