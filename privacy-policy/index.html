Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

OM4BIZ respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect personal information through om4biz.com, our website forms and communications relating to our services.

Company and contact details are available on our Contact page.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact information: your name, email address, telephone number and preferred contact method.
  • Business information: your business name, website, role, services and information you provide about your requirements.
  • Enquiry and assessment information: form submissions, correspondence, consultation arrangements and materials you voluntarily share.
  • Transaction information: billing details, payment status, transaction references and records relating to quotations, invoices or refunds.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser and device information, pages visited and website security logs, where collected by our hosting or website tools.

Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, sensitive personal information or identifiable customer records through general enquiry forms.

2. How we use information

We use information as needed to:

  • Respond to enquiries and assessment requests.
  • Understand your requirements and prepare proposals.
  • Arrange consultations and deliver agreed services.
  • Send service updates, confirmations and other communications relevant to your request.
  • Administer payments, invoices and refunds.
  • Maintain website functionality, prevent misuse and address security issues.
  • Meet applicable legal, accounting and recordkeeping requirements.
  • Resolve complaints and disputes.

We do not treat an enquiry or assessment request as permission to send unrelated promotional messages.

3. Consent and other permitted processing

Where consent is required, we request it for the relevant purpose. Where applicable law permits, we may also process information to fulfil an agreement, respond to a request you initiate, comply with legal obligations or protect against fraud and misuse.

You may withdraw consent through the contact channels on our Contact page. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out lawfully. Some information may still need to be retained or processed where permitted or required by law.

If information necessary to respond to your request or deliver a service is withheld, we may be unable to complete that request or service.

4. Communications

We contact you using the details and preferences you provide. Communications may take place through email, telephone, WhatsApp or another channel agreed with you.

If you separately subscribe to marketing updates, you can unsubscribe using the option in the message or contact us to withdraw your subscription. Declining marketing does not prevent you from requesting our services.

Third-party communication platforms handle information under their own privacy policies.

5. Cookies and website technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functionality, security and, where enabled, measurement of website use.

Where non-essential analytics or advertising technologies are used, we provide information about them and obtain consent where required before activating them.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings and any consent controls provided on the website. Blocking essential technologies may affect website functionality.

6. Payments

Where online payments are offered, they may be handled by a third-party payment provider. That provider processes payment information according to its own privacy policy.

OM4BIZ may receive billing information, payment status and transaction references needed to administer your purchase. Do not send full card numbers, security codes or banking passwords through our forms or messages.

7. When we share information

We may share relevant information with:

  • Service providers supporting website hosting, communications, scheduling, payments, recordkeeping or agreed service delivery.
  • Authorised team members and contractors who need access to carry out their work.
  • Professional advisers where necessary for accounting, legal or compliance purposes.
  • Authorities or other parties where disclosure is legally required or necessary to address fraud, security incidents or legal claims.

We limit sharing to information relevant to the purpose and apply appropriate confidentiality and data-handling requirements.

We do not sell personal information.

Information is not automatically shared with other businesses or ventures featured on our website merely because you contact OM4BIZ. A referral involving your information requires an appropriate basis and, where required, your consent.

8. International processing

Our team and service providers may process or store information in countries other than your country of residence.

Where applicable, we use legally required safeguards for international transfers. Privacy laws and protections may differ between countries.

9. Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including service delivery, follow-up, legal obligations and dispute resolution.

Retention depends on the type of information and the relationship involved. Enquiry information, project records and financial records may have different retention periods.

When information is no longer required, we delete it or anonymise it, subject to applicable requirements and normal backup cycles.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure. Access is restricted according to operational need.

No website, communication channel or storage system can guarantee absolute security. If a personal-data incident occurs, we will take appropriate action and provide notifications where required by applicable law.

11. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of information.
  • Withdraw consent.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Receive information in a transferable format, where applicable.
  • Raise a privacy complaint with us or a relevant authority.

Send requests using the contact channels on our Contact page and identify your request as a Privacy Request.

We may need proportionate information to verify your identity before acting. We respond within applicable legal timeframes and explain any lawful limitations on fulfilling a request.

12. Information about other people

If you provide information about colleagues, customers or other individuals, ensure you are authorised to share it and have supplied any required notices or obtained necessary permissions.

For assessments, use anonymised examples wherever possible. Personal information about your customers is generally unnecessary for reviewing a booking journey.

Where a project requires us to process personal information on your behalf, additional contractual data-protection terms may be needed.

13. Children

Our website and business services are intended for adults acting in a professional capacity. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

14. Third-party websites

Our website may link to external websites, platforms or venture websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

This policy covers OM4BIZ’s website and the activities described here; it does not replace the privacy policies of those external services.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our services, systems or legal requirements. The revised policy will be published here with an updated date.

Where a change requires additional notice or fresh consent, we will provide it as required. Updating this policy does not override your applicable privacy rights.

16. Contact us

For privacy questions, requests or complaints, use the contact details on our Contact page and mark your message Privacy Request.
