Case study

D6 Kitchens — pivoting through disruption

Challenge

D6 Kitchens launched in January 2020 as a managed cloud kitchen — weeks before the pandemic disrupted the F&B sector's original operating model.

Response

The business pivoted toward a technology-enabled, multi-brand model — proprietary in-house technology running multiple food brands from one kitchen.

Outcome

D6K now operates as a multi-brand cloud kitchen spanning online delivery and takeaway, with OM4Biz providing F&B management consultancy under the same brand.
