FAQs

Before you start.

Does this include a working website?

No. The assessment delivers recommendations, a proposed flow, a visual mockup of one key booking screen and an implementation proposal. A working website or booking system, interactive prototypes and complete app designs are separate engagements.

Do I have to proceed with OM4BIZ?

No. Use the assessment to plan your next steps; implementation is optional and separately agreed. If you accept the implementation proposal and pay the agreed project deposit within 30 calendar days of receiving it, the AED 499 assessment fee is deducted once from the implementation service fee. The credit has no cash value and excludes hosting, subscriptions and other third-party charges.

Will this guarantee more bookings?

We commit to the stated assessment deliverables. Booking results depend on customer demand, traffic, pricing, availability, service quality and implementation, so the assessment does not guarantee revenue or booking increases.

What do you need from me, and when is it delivered?

Your website address, main service list, pricing or quoting approach, an explanation of your booking process and brand assets where available. We don’t need customer passwords or identifiable customer records. Delivery is within three working days after your consultation and receipt of the required information (Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays observed by OM4BIZ); we confirm the date once both are complete.
